Tôi đã dẫn dắt hơn 60 sáng kiến chuyển đổi số và quản lý các hệ thống phức tạp: ERP SAP S/4HANA, Quản lý chuỗi cung ứng, Sản xuất tích hợp (MES) và Kho tự động (ASRS).

Tôi mang đến hiểu biết thực tiễn sâu rộng trong các ngành Sản xuất, Dược phẩm, Xây dựng, Bán lẻ và Hàng tiêu dùng — từ kiến trúc doanh nghiệp đến tự động hóa quy trình bằng AI.

Alongside enterprise delivery, I pursue academic research in AI, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Edge AI and IoT — with work published in IEEE journals and open-sourced on GitHub. Song song với công việc doanh nghiệp, tôi nghiên cứu học thuật về AI, Học máy, Thị giác máy tính, Edge AI và IoT — với công bố trên tạp chí IEEE và mã nguồn mở trên GitHub.

Q1 IEEE Xplore Peer-reviewed journal articleBài báo tạp chí phản biện

Certifiable Worst-Case Detection Latency for Energy-Efficient Frame-Skipping at the Edge

Dat Lam Quoc · IEEE Internet of Things Journal · Early Access, October 2026Early Access, 10/2026
DOI: 10.1109/JIOT.2026.3740931
AbstractTóm tắt

Edge video-analytics systems on Internet-of-Things devices skip the detector on most frames to save energy, but never bound how long a real event can go undetected: they are validated empirically, never certified. We give a framework that makes “safe to skip” provable. We derive a deterministic worst-case bound on missed-event latency for any refresh-bounded policy; we characterize the stochastic latency law under random event phase, uniform latency with a distribution-free miss probability set by the measured duration law, and show surveillance durations are heavy-tailed, so mean-based closed forms badly mispredict misses; and we prove an energy–miss frontier on which periodic refresh is the optimal oblivious policy, pricing certifiability as the energy to guarantee events above a chosen duration. The random-phase assumption can be enforced rather than assumed: a randomized-phase refresh makes the law hold by construction even against adversarially synchronized events, and dependence-robust budgets replace independence. On 121 videos across three datasets the theory holds, and a one-frame off-by-one leaks the certificate exactly where theory predicts, restored by a hard refresh. A deployed motion gate’s advantage at equal energy is marginal and alignment-dependent; we give the correlation threshold a content-aware gate must clear, and find gains obey an alignment–density law from 76% to 1% event density. Measurements across platforms show the energy ratio κ belongs to the stack, not the silicon, spanning 5.9 to 66 on a single board and reaching 269 on a microcontroller, where a platform floor caps absolute savings near 25%, setting the price of certifiability.
